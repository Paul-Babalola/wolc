## Overview

A look at what it means to keep in step with the Spirit in everyday life.

## Key points

- Walking in the Spirit is a daily, moment-by-moment choice.
- The fruit of the Spirit grows as we stay connected to God.
- Grace empowers what willpower alone cannot.
